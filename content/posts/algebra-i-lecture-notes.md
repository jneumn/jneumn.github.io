After a long time, I have finally finished polishing and cleaning up my lecture notes for the introductory Algebra I course at the [University of Vienna](https://www.univie.ac.at/), taught by [Harald Grobner](https://homepage.univie.ac.at/harald.grobner/).

The contents include the basics of group, ring, and field theory, including the basics of Galois theory and a rather long detour into algebraic number theory (including open results and some more in-depth discussion).

Please feel free to take a look and do not hesitate to contact me in case you find any typos or errors, as minor as they may be.
